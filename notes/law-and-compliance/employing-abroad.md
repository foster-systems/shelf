title: Employing people in other countries
description: "The rules that decide which country's labour law, social security and tax apply when a company's staff work from another country, and when a contractor counts as an employee."
why: "A remote team often has engineers in countries where the company has no office. Their country's mandatory labour law protects them whatever the contract says, and they are usually insured for social security where they work. A home office can also give the company a taxable presence in that country, and a contractor who works like an employee can be reclassified as one."
